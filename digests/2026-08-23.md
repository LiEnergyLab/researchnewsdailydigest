This study provides direct spectroscopic evidence that CO2 reduction on copper in a flow cell proceeds via an Eley–Rideal mechanism, where CO2 from the electrolyte reacts directly with an adsorbed hydrogen atom, rather than the traditionally assumed Langmuir–Hinshelwood pathway.
