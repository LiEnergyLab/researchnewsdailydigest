### [Decoupling the Impact of Surface Functionalization over Pt at the Cathode on Kinetics and Mass Transport in PEMFCs](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c11994)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEMFC · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-17 · **Authors:** Linke Fu, Sishan Feng, Lulu Tang, Kaiyue Zhao, Zixuan Yu, Qiwen Sun

Treating the cathode Pt surface with the small molecule 7-ethyltheophylline (ETP) before adding the ionomer improves fuel-cell performance by both displacing poisoning sulfonate groups and reducing oxygen transport resistance. The effect is decoupled: peak kinetic activity (~50% gain) is achieved at a low 0.15% loading, while transport resistance keeps dropping as loading rises to 1.0%, meaning H₂-air operation requires a different optimal loading than H₂-O₂. This shows that surface modifiers can independently tune kinetics and mass transport, providing a new lever for optimizing PEMFC cathodes beyond conventional catalyst design.

Using molecular dynamics simulations, this study shows that CO2 transport in amine-loaded MCM-41 DAC sorbents is governed by a trade-off between polymer mobility and steric obstruction, not by polymer mobility alone.
