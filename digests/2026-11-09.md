### [Water-Transport Resistance of Perfluorosulfonic-Acid (PFSA) Membranes: Delineating the Role of Interface and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c02544)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM-membranes · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Catherine M. Weiss, Shouwen Shi, Adam Z. Weber, Ahmet Kusoglu

Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance drops as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for medium side-chain chemistry (3M), with water content the dominant controlling variable. Across a 12-chemistry/thickness × 16-condition dataset, transport resistance fell with temperature and especially RH (order-of-magnitude larger effect), and interfacial resistance showed substantially higher activation energy than bulk. The results give a mechanistic basis for tuning side-chain chemistry to balance water transport against proton conductivity.

PFSA ionomer adsorption onto IrOx in electrolyzer inks depends on the free-ionomer concentration and solvent composition, not on catalyst loading or the ionomer-to-catalyst ratio.
