### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR-acidic · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max Joshua Hülsey, Bryan Yuk-Wah Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

CO2 reduction on coinage metals in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted, meaning a local pH swing is a prerequisite for the reaction rather than a consequence of it. Using DEMS to separate faradaic from non-faradaic CO2 consumption, the authors show that added cations act mainly by creating a hydronium-depleted interfacial environment, and that promoting H2 evolution (Pt-doped Ag) lowers the CO2RR onset overpotential by ~1 V. They propose that acidic local pH favors *COOH, which fragments unproductively to CO2 and H* instead of proceeding to CO.
