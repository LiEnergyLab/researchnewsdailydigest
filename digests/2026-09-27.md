### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR-acidic-electrolytes · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max Joshua Hülsey, Bryan Yuk-Wah Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

CO2 reduction on coinage metals in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted, meaning a local pH swing is a prerequisite for the reaction rather than a side effect. The authors show this by simultaneously tracking faradaic CO2 consumption and non-faradaic (bi)carbonate formation with DEMS, and demonstrate that promoting hydronium consumption (Pt-doped Ag) lowers the CO2RR onset overpotential by ~1 V. They propose that at low local pH the *COOH intermediate fragments unproductively back to CO2 and H*, favoring H2 evolution over CO2RR.
