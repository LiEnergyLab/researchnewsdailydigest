**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** electrolyzer scale-up · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-03 · **Authors:** Ang Lu, Danji Huang, Qunlei Wen, Shichang Cui, Wenxin Liu, Hao Li

A unified stack-scale multiphysics model comparing industrial AWE, AEM, and PEM stacks finds that AWE suffers the strongest intercell nonuniformity, driven by shunt-current redistribution causing a 12.38% rated current loss and 7.21% efficiency drop, while AEM and PEM losses are dominated instead by gas-phase and temperature effects. ### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** acidic CO2RR · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max Joshua Hülsey, Bryan Yuk-Wah Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

Using real-time DEMS with scanning voltammetry on coinage metals, the authors show that CO₂ reduction in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted—i.e., a local pH swing is a prerequisite for, not a result of, CO₂RR. Promoting hydronium consumption (e.g., Pt-doped Ag) lowers the CO₂RR onset overpotential by ~1 V, and the authors attribute the inhibition at low pH to protonated *COOH fragmenting unproductively to CO₂ + H* instead of proceeding to CO.
