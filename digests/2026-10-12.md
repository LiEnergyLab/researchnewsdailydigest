### [Self-Catalyzed Hastelloy Fiber Paper (HAP) Porous Transport Layer for Oxygen Evolution Reaction in Anion Exchange Membrane Water Electrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.langmuir.6c04347)

Hastelloy fiber paper used directly as the anode porous transport layer—with no catalyst coating—supports 725.67 mA cm⁻² at 1.8 V and 70 °C in an AEM electrolyzer, and still 701.34 mA cm⁻² under cathode-dry operation, with stability over 5000 AST cycles. ### [Water Activation as a Limiting Factor for High Energy Efficiency in CO2 Electroreduction](https://doi.org/10.1021/acselectrochem.6c00222)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electroreduction · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-29 · **Authors:** José Guillermo Rivera de la Cruz, Jonathan Raisin, Marc Fontecave, Dong Un Lee, Thomas Francisco Jaramillo, Tiras Y. Lin

Interfacial proton depletion under industrial current densities imposes a net thermodynamic voltage penalty of ~0.6 V in zero-gap MEA CO2 electrolyzers, setting a practical energy floor for C2+ production. The authors argue this penalty can only be avoided by synchronizing interfacial proton delivery with dimerization kinetics in acidic systems, which remains unproven at high rates.
