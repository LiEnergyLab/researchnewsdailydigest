### [Multi-fidelity computational screening of high-entropy MBenes for electrochemical CO2 reduction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-026-02287-4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR MBenes · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-17 · **Authors:** Sree Harsha Bharadwaj H, Raghavan Ranganathan

A multi-fidelity computational screen of 56 quinary high-entropy MBenes identified 18 viable CO2 reduction catalysts, with three compositions (CrNbZrMoTiB5, MoZrHfNbCrB5, MoZrHfTaCrB5) showing spontaneous CO2-to-CO conversion at zero applied potential, outperforming previously reported MBene and high-entropy-alloy catalysts. The screening, which used DFT, formation-energy filtering, and a MACE machine-learning potential across 1375 surface environments, attributes the activity to Hf/Zr-mediated electron donation to Cr active sites that stabilizes the key COOH* intermediate. This work provides a transferable framework for discovering multicomponent 2D electrocatalysts.
