### [Dataset for "Large Language Model-Guided Screening of N-Containing Molecule-Modified Cu-Based Catalysts for Acidic CO2 Electroreduction"](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34908588)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** acidic CO2RR · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-04 · **Authors:** Cheng Wang, Enyu He

An LLM-guided, human-in-the-loop workflow screened 110 nitrogen-containing molecular modifiers for Cu catalysts in acidic CO2 electroreduction across four rounds (666 data points), finding that C2+ selectivity depends on alkyl-chain architecture combined with amine configuration. ### [Water-Transport Resistance of Perfluorosulfonic-Acid (PFSA) Membranes: Delineating the Role of Interface and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c02544)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM-membranes · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Catherine M. Weiss, Shouwen Shi, Adam Z. Weber, Ahmet Kusoglu

Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance drops as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for medium side-chain lengths (3M), so no single chemistry minimizes both. Across 12 chemistry-thickness combinations and 16 humidity/temperature conditions, water content was the dominant controlling variable, with RH having roughly an order-of-magnitude greater impact than temperature. Activation-energy analysis confirms interfacial transport needs substantially more thermal energy than bulk transport, giving a mechanistic basis for tuning membrane chemistry against the water-transport/conductivity trade-off.
