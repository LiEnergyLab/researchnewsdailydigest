### [Managing Alkaline CO2 Electrolyzer Flooding using a Grooved Cathode Gas Diffusion Electrode](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c01086)

A grooved cathode GDE with through-plane edge holes reduced CO Faradaic efficiency loss from 38.4% to 18.1% over 6 h at 250 mA/cm² under wet CO2, and from 35.5% to 29.8% under dry CO2, by draining anolyte and lowering cathode mass-transport resistance (0.62 vs 1.3 Ω·cm²). ### [Decoupling the Impact of Surface Functionalization over Pt at the Cathode on Kinetics and Mass Transport in PEMFCs](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c11994)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEMFC cathode · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-17 · **Authors:** Linke Fu, Sishan Feng, Lulu Tang, Kaiyue Zhao, Zixuan Yu, Qiwen Sun

Adsorbing 7-ethyltheophylline on Pt before adding Nafion displaces poisoning sulfonate groups and boosts peak mass activity by ~50% (0.33 A mgPt⁻¹) at just 0.15% loading, while oxygen transport resistance keeps falling up to 1.0% loading. Because kinetics and mass transport peak at different coverages, H₂–O₂ and H₂–air operation have distinct optimal loadings. The practical takeaway: interface modifiers can be tuned separately for kinetics versus transport, so catalyst-layer optimization should not assume a single optimum.

### [Impact of the OER Activity and Stability of IrO 2 Anode Co–Catalysts on the Durability of Membrane Electrode Assemblies under Transient Operation Conditions of a PEM Fuel Cell](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/ae97bb)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEMFC MEA durability · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-09 · **Authors:** Leonardo Isaias Astudillo, Hubert A. Gasteiger, Mohammad Fathi Tovini

Two IrO₂ anode co-catalysts with different susceptibility to reduction in H₂ were tested in MEAs under combined start-up/shut-down and cell-reversal cycling. The more easily reduced, higher-OER-activity catalyst dissolved more iridium and lost more performance early in the test, with its activity advantage only emerging later. The results indicate that reduction resistance, not just OER activity, should be a primary screening criterion for anode co-catalysts, with the best trade-off depending on the operating profile.

### [Distribution and Transport of CO2 in Hyperbranched Poly(Alkylene Imine)-Loaded MCM-41: A Molecular Dynamics Simulation Approach](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.energyfuels.6c03434)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** DAC sorbents · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-26 · **Authors:** Junhe Chen, Guilherme R. Weber Nakamura, Christopher W. Jones, Sung Hyun Kwon, Seung Soon Jang

MD simulations of hyperbranched PEI- and PPI-loaded MCM-41 under humid DAC conditions show that CO2 accumulates at polymer–void interfaces and diffuses anisotropically along the mesopore axis, with mobility depending nonmonotonically on humidity due to competing polymer plasticization and water blocking. Optimal loadings were ~34 wt% (HB-PEI) and ~37 wt% (HB-PPI), and secondary amines dominate local coordination of both CO2 and H2O. Notably, the more mobile HB-PPI is not the better CO2 conductor—HB-PEI maintains higher diffusivity under humid conditions because its more compact organization reduces transient steric obstruction.

### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR acidic · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max J. Hülsey, Bryan Y. Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

Using real-time DEMS to separate faradaic from non-faradaic CO₂ consumption, the authors show that CO₂ reduction on coinage metals in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted—i.e., a local pH swing is a prerequisite, not a consequence, of CO₂RR. Promoting hydronium consumption (e.g., Pt-doped Ag) lowers the CO₂RR onset overpotential by ~1 V, and they propose that acidic interfacial pH favors *COOH, which fragments unproductively to CO₂ + H* rather than proceeding to CO.

### [Unifying Water Oxidation Chemistry of Iridium Oxide from Aqueous Model Systems to Proton Exchange Membrane Electrolysers](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15008370/v1)

Using operando X-ray and time-resolved optical spectroscopy on identical IrOx catalysts, the authors find that Nafion ionomer does not change the intrinsic redox transitions or electrophilic oxygen species responsible for O–O bond formation—it mainly affects mass transport via oxygen trapping and microbubbles.
