### [Water-Transport Resistance of Perfluorosulfonic-Acid (PFSA) Membranes: Delineating the Role of Interface and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c02544)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM-membrane · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Catherine M. Weiss, Shouwen Shi, Adam Z. Weber, Ahmet Kusoglu

Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance drops as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for medium side-chain chemistry (3M), so no single chemistry minimizes both. Water content is the dominant controlling variable, with RH having roughly an order of magnitude more impact than temperature, and interfacial transport needs substantially more thermal energy to overcome than bulk transport. The systematic 12-chemistry/thickness × 16-condition dataset gives a mechanistic basis for trading off water transport against conductivity in membrane design.
