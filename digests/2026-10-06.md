A unified stack-scale multiphysics model comparing industrial AWE, AEM, and PEM stacks finds that AWE suffers the worst intercell nonuniformity, driven by shunt-current redistribution causing a 12.38% rated current loss and 7.21% efficiency drop, while AEM and PEM losses are dominated instead by gas-phase and temperature effects on voltage efficiency. Combining Fe K-edge XAS, ⁵⁷Fe Mössbauer spectroscopy, and DFT-based spectral simulations, the authors resolve the Fe-site distribution in a pyrolyzed Fe–N–C ORR catalyst as predominantly a defect-associated pyrrolic FeN₄ motif with third-shell nitrogen coordination, plus a smaller pyridinic fraction.
