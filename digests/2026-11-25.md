### [Preferential nucleation sites regulate hydrogen bubble evolution on mesh electrodes](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6fd00127k)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** electrode bubble dynamics · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** Kensei Tsuburaya, Akash Raman, Stefan Schlautmann, Keisuke Obata, Kazuhiro Takanabe, David Fernández Rivas

Pt-coated silicon mesh cathodes with hydrophobic pits as preferential nucleation sites were fabricated and compared against pit-free and conventional Ni meshes, showing that pit placement and density measurably change H2 bubble growth rate and coverage fraction. Faces without pits retained large bubbles at mesh apertures, indicating that nucleation-site engineering can alter bubble residence behavior on practical 3D electrode geometries. The work is a platform/methodology contribution; it does not report electrolysis efficiency gains.

This review argues that high-altitude PEMFC performance loss stems mainly from reduced oxygen partial pressure, which worsens activation and concentration polarization and raises compressor load, cutting net power. ### [Water-Transport Resistance of Perfluorosulfonic-Acid (PFSA) Membranes: Delineating the Role of Interface and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c02544)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM membrane water transport · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Catherine M. Weiss, Shouwen Shi, Adam Z. Weber, Ahmet Kusoglu

Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance drops as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for the medium side-chain (3M) chemistry. Across a 12-membrane × 16-condition dataset, water content (λ) is the controlling variable, with RH having roughly an order of magnitude more impact than temperature, and interfacial transport requires substantially higher activation energy than bulk. The results give a mechanistic basis for tuning side-chain chemistry against the water-transport/conductivity trade-off.

### [Effects of pressure on the oxygen evolution reaction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.elecom.2026.108280)

Operating a three-electrode alkaline cell at 1–20 bar increases OER current mainly by mitigating bubble-related non-kinetic losses, with the enhancement scaling linearly with 1/p (i.e., with volumetric gas flux) under transient cyclic voltammetry. ### [Beyond Faradaic Efficiency: Towards efficient electroreduction of CO2 in Membrane-Electrode assemblies](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mattod.2026.103537)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR MEA · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-27 · **Authors:** Huali Wu, Yan Jiao, Damien Voiry

This review argues that scaling CO2 electroreduction in membrane-electrode assemblies is a multi-physics problem—mechanical compression, mass transport, and thermal dynamics—not just geometric enlargement, and that the solid–gas interface microenvironment (modulated via ionomer functionalization, hierarchical structuring, and tandem catalysis) critically governs performance and selectivity. Feasible pathways include segmented flow fields, thermally/hydraulically balanced stacks, and precise water/thermal management.
