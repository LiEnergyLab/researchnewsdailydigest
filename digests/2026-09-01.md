### [Silver‐Modulated Copper Dynamics Under Pulsed Tandem Electrochemical CO 2 Reduction](https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.75324)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 reduction · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-29 · **Authors:** Blaž Tomc, Rajeena Uruniyengal, Matjaž Finšgar, Mitja Kostelec, Matic Plut, Piotr Dobroń

Pulsed operation of a Cu-Ag catalyst for CO₂ reduction does not preserve the initial structure; instead, it drives in-situ restructuring into a dynamic state that boosts CO production and maintains ethylene while suppressing hydrogen, methane, and formate.
