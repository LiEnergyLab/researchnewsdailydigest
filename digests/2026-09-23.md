MD simulations of hyperbranched PEI- and PPI-loaded MCM-41 under humid DAC conditions show CO2 diffuses anisotropically along the mesopore axis and that its mobility depends nonmonotonically on humidity, reflecting a trade-off between polymer plasticization and water-induced steric blocking.
