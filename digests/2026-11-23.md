### [Electrolysis without electrodes: thermodynamic and kinetic insights into decoupled alkaline water electrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6fd00117c)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** decoupled water electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** Mark Potter, Luís Pinho, Kathryn Ellen Toghill

Using dissolved redox mediators to decouple electron transfer from catalysis, the authors achieved hydrogen evolution at potentials approaching the thermodynamic limit in strongly alkaline media—even on nickel, not just platinum. Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance falls as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for the medium side-chain (3M) chemistry.
