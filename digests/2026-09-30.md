### [Variable Rate Hydroxylamine Derivatization to Determine of Formaldehyde’s Role and Kinetics in CO2 Reduction to Methanol on Cobalt Phthalocyanine](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15009504/v1)

Trapping formaldehyde with fast hydroxylamine derivatives during CO2 reduction on CoPc/CNT cuts methanol selectivity by about 25%, indicating that free formaldehyde is a short-lived intermediate accounting for only a quarter of the methanol produced. Using identical IrOx catalysts across three-electrode, catalyst–ionomer, and MEA configurations, the authors show that Nafion ionomer does not change the intrinsic Ir redox transitions or the electrophilic oxygen species responsible for O–O bond formation—it mainly affects mass transport via oxygen trapping and microbubble accumulation.
