Constant-potential DFT plus microkinetic modeling shows formate formation on Cu, Au, and Pb proceeds through different dominant pathways: O-bound CO₂ protonation on Cu, kinetically unfavorable facet/potential-dependent routes on Au, and direct outer-sphere conversion of physisorbed CO₂ on Pb. A specification-driven LLM agent can build complete open-source Julia multiphysics models of a CO₂-to-CO gas diffusion electrode that match an equivalent COMSOL implementation to within 0.7% of peak CO partial current density, with independently built models agreeing to 0.04%. The approach keeps governing equations and parameters as human-specified inputs while the agent handles implementation, and planted-error tests show explicit specifications are needed for reproducibility and debugging.

### [The Stability of Rutile Oxides for Oxygen Evolution Catalysis: From Mechanistic Understanding to New Descriptors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12297v1)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER catalysts · **Journal:** arXiv · **Published:** 2026-10-08 · **Authors:** Katarina Kretschmer, Michael H. Eikerling, Tobias Binninger

A computational analysis attributes the lower stability of rutile RuO2 versus IrO2 to differences in the covalent versus ionic character of their metal–oxygen bonds, quantified via crystal-orbital-derived electronic descriptors. The authors propose these descriptors as a route to screening more durable OER catalysts. The work is purely electronic-structure modeling, so any predictive stability claims remain to be validated experimentally.
