### [Linker Functionalization and pH Tuning Enhance Solar-Driven Catalytic CO$_2$ Reduction in MOF-5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02256v1)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** MOF CO2 photoreduction · **Journal:** arXiv · **Published:** 2026-09-02 · **Authors:** Julia Santana-Andreo, Joshua Edzards, Surender Kumar, Caterina Cocchi

In MOF-5, linker functionalization—not strain or metal substitution—effectively narrows the band gap into the visible range while preserving the needed reduction energetics, with the COOH-functionalized series offering the broadest thermodynamic access to full CO₂ reduction products. The choice of metal node in this series tunes the gap by over 1 eV with minimal impact on overpotentials, making Sr- and Ba-based variants the most favorable for visible-light-driven, product-selective catalysis. Overall, linker chemistry and solution pH act as independent levers to separately optimize light absorption and reduction thermodynamics.

### [Cascade coalescence dynamically sustains bubble retention near gas-evolving surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03387v1)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Tag:** bubble dynamics electrolysis · **Journal:** arXiv · **Published:** 2026-09-03 · **Authors:** Tao Wu, Bo Liu, Haohao Hao, Xuehua Zhang, Fang Yuan, Huanshu Tan, Qiang Yang

Gas-evolving electrodes can retain bubbles even at high gas flux, and this work identifies the cause: cascade coalescence between unequal-sized bubbles. When a rising bubble merges with a smaller surface-attached one, asymmetric interfacial retraction generates a net downward viscous impulse that reverses the bubble's trajectory toward the surface. Repeated events accumulate into a retaining force that can oppose buoyancy by 3–4 orders of magnitude beyond static limits, establishing coalescence as a key dynamic mechanism for bubble retention.

### [Optimizing diffusion-limited transport, with applications to electrochemical systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00849v1)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Tag:** electrochemical transport · **Journal:** arXiv · **Published:** 2026-09-01 · **Authors:** Manu Mannattil, L. Mahadevan

For a given concentration limit at an electrode, the optimal charging or discharging protocol is to apply maximum current until that limit is hit, then gradually taper the current to hold the boundary concentration steady. This "bang-ride" strategy is shown analytically to outperform constant-current operation, with the improvement bounded by a system-independent factor for semi-infinite domains. The framework is generalized to finite and multilayer geometries, covering both charge and discharge cases.

### [Repairing PBE-Spurious Metallicity for HSE06-Level Screening of 2D Photocatalysts for Green Hydrogen Production](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02883v1)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Tag:** photocatalyst screening · **Journal:** arXiv · **Published:** 2026-09-02 · **Authors:** Ritam Chakraborty, Arpan Das

PBE calculations falsely label many narrow-gap 2D semiconductors as metals, which can wrongly eliminate them from photocatalyst screens. A two-stage machine-learning workflow that repairs this spurious metallicity and learns the HSE06-PBE gap correction reconstructs HSE06 band gaps with a mean absolute error of 0.108 eV, versus 1.036 eV for raw PBE. Applying this correction changes the final candidate list for green-hydrogen photocatalysts, showing that screening outcomes, not just global error metrics, determine the value of the correction.
