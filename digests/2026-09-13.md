### [Consequences of overtones in Raman spectra for assigning nickel anode surface structures as NiO 2 during alkaline electrolysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-026-01613-9)

### [Distribution and Transport of CO2 in Hyperbranched Poly(Alkylene Imine)-Loaded MCM-41: A Molecular Dynamics Simulation Approach](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.energyfuels.6c03434)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** DAC amine sorbents · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-26 · **Authors:** Junhe Chen, Guilherme R. Weber Nakamura, Christopher W. Jones, Sung Hyun Kwon, Seung Soon Jang

MD simulations of hyperbranched PEI- and PPI-loaded MCM-41 under humid DAC conditions show CO2 diffuses anisotropically along the mesopore axis and that its mobility depends nonmonotonically on humidity, reflecting a trade-off between polymer plasticization and water-induced steric blocking. **Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER NiOOH electrolyte · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-09 · **Authors:** Jordy J. J. Eggebeen, Thomas Touzalin, Yaxin Liu, Onno van der Heijden, Alfred Larsson, Marc T. M. Koper

The apparent pH dependence of OER on NiOOH is largely a transport artifact: activity rises with alkalinity and depends on cation identity, but is independent of bulk cation concentration, which instead only improves Ni(OH)₂/NiOOH redox reversibility via mesopore ion transport. Thick, heterogeneous films show the strongest pH effect due to diffusion limitations, while thin homogeneous layers show little. Catalyst mesostructure, not intrinsic pH kinetics, sets how strongly these effects appear.
