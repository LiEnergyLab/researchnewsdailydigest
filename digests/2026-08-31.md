The paper argues that designing CO₂ electrolyzers to tolerate dilute or impure feeds simply shifts the separation burden downstream, where it becomes more difficult and costly. ### [Decoupling the Impact of Surface Functionalization over Pt at the Cathode on Kinetics and Mass Transport in PEMFCs](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c11994)

Treating the Pt cathode with the small molecule 7-ethyltheophylline (ETP) before adding Nafion improves fuel-cell performance by both displacing poisoning sulfonate groups and activating the Pt surface. PFSA ionomer adsorption onto catalyst particles is electrostatically self-limiting, with adsorption ceasing once the particle surface reaches a common negative charge density of about 25 kBT barrier, regardless of particle type.
