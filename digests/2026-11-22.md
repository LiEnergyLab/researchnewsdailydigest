Engineered cracks in the catalyst layer of PEMWE membrane electrode assemblies improved current density by up to 35% at a given voltage for 50% and 100% stretched MEAs, while a 75% stretch performed worse due to reduced through-plane proton conductivity of the Nafion membrane. **Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt formation · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance across the cathode chamber shows that inlet CO2 from a water bubbler is often undersaturated, so evaporation lowers the local liquid water-to-K+ ratio and drives bicarbonate precipitation—even when anolyte crossover supplies enough water in principle.
