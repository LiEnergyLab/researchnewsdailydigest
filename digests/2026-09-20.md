### [Revisiting Electrolyte Effects in Oxygen Evolution Reaction on NiOOH: pH‐Controlled Activity Depends on Catalyst Mesostructure](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.70300)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER electrocatalysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-09 · **Authors:** Jordy J. J. Eggebeen, Thomas Touzalin, Yaxin Liu, Onno van der Heijden, Alfred Larsson, Marc T. M. Koper

Decoupling pH from cation concentration shows that OER activity on NiOOH rises with alkalinity and depends on cation identity but not bulk cation concentration; cation concentration only improves Ni(OH)₂/NiOOH redox reversibility via mesopore ion transport.
