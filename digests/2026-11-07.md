### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance across all cathode-chamber sources and phases shows that inlet CO₂ from a water bubbler is often undersaturated, so evaporation inside the cathode lowers the local liquid water-to-K⁺ ratio enough to precipitate bicarbonate salts—even when anolyte crossover water would otherwise suffice to dissolve the cotransported K⁺. Excessive humidification prevents salting but causes electrode flooding, indicating a trade-off between dehydration-driven crystallization and overhumidification-driven transport limits. The work frames cathode water management as the governing variable for salt control in CO₂ electrolyzers.
