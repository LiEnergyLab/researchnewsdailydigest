### [Water-Transport Resistance of Perfluorosulfonic-Acid (PFSA) Membranes: Delineating the Role of Interface and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c02544)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM membrane water transport · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Catherine M. Weiss, Shouwen Shi, Adam Z. Weber, Ahmet Kusoglu

Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance drops as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for medium side-chain chemistry (3M), so no single chemistry minimizes both. Across a 12-chemistry/thickness × 16-condition dataset, water content is the dominant controlling variable, with RH having roughly an order of magnitude more impact than temperature. Interfacial transport also requires substantially higher activation energy than bulk, supporting separate design levers for interface versus bulk in membrane tailoring.
