Interfacial proton depletion under industrial current densities imposes a net thermodynamic voltage penalty of ~0.6 V in zero-gap MEA CO2 electrolyzers, even after accounting for the pH-gradient "discount," setting a practical energy floor for direct CO2-to-C2+ conversion. Until then, they conclude a sequential CO2-to-CO then CO-to-C2+ cascade is the more robust industrial route.
