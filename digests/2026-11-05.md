### [Hydrogen Crossover in AEM Water Electrolysis: Influence of Cell Architecture and Catalyst Layer Supersaturation](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/aebd20)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** AEM water electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-03 · **Authors:** Naveen Guruprasad, John Van der Schaaf, Matheus Theodorus de Groot

Using identical MEAs in 1 M KOH, two zero-gap cell architectures differed in hydrogen crossover by 2.9-fold, traced by a 1D transport model to catalyst-layer supersaturation rather than membrane properties; a hydrophobic carbon GDE cathode cut crossover by over an order of magnitude versus the commercial platform. ### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt formation · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance for CO2 electrolyzer cathodes shows that inlet CO2 through a water bubbler is often not fully humidified, so evaporation inside the cathode chamber lowers the local liquid water-to-K+ ratio and drives bicarbonate salt precipitation—even when anolyte crossover water would otherwise suffice to dissolve the cotransported K+. The finding frames cathode water management as the governing variable for salt control and stability.
