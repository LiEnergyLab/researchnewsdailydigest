### [Techno-Economic and Life Cycle Assessment for a Hydrogen Production System Using Renewable Energy](https://doi.org/10.32604/ee.2026.082598)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** H2-techno-economics-LCA · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** Yutong Lei, Zhongchao Yang, Xingyu Liu, Mingtao Tang, Mingchen Yao

A 100 MW alkaline electrolysis system modeled in Aspen Plus shows a life-cycle carbon footprint of 1.94 kg CO2eq/kg H2 on PV power (91% from electrolyzer electricity) and an LCOH of 25.403 CNY/kg, with 56.57% energy and 55.61% exergy efficiency. The results support prioritizing low-cost, low-carbon electricity and high capacity factors for distributed green hydrogen.

### [Balancing Hydration of Anion-Exchange Membranes for Ion Transport and Interfacial Stability in CO2 Electrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.6c02315)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** AEM-CO2-electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-22 · **Authors:** Jisoo Park, Hyungrae Kim, Insu Jeong, Jung Kyu Kim, Jai Hyun Koh

A poly(biphenyl piperidine) AEM with an ion-exchange capacity of 2.3 meq/g gave the best CO2 electrolysis performance (83.5% CO Faradaic efficiency at 200 mA/cm² versus 48.4% at 1.5 meq/g), while the more highly charged 2.5 meq/g membrane lost stability within 2 hours due to overhydration. The key variable was "transport-effective hydration"—interconnected hydrophilic morphology plus a high fraction of freezable water—rather than charge density alone. This suggests AEM design should target an optimal hydration state rather than maximum IEC.

### [Dynamic Electrocatalysis Enhances the Oxygen Evolution Reaction Activity of Iridium Oxides](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.70341)

Square-wave potential modulation of iridium oxide anodes raises acidic OER activity by up to 480% versus static operation. ### [Water-Transport Resistance of Perfluorosulfonic-Acid (PFSA) Membranes: Delineating the Role of Interface and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c02544)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM water transport · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Catherine M. Weiss, Shouwen Shi, Adam Z. Weber, Ahmet Kusoglu

Interfacial and bulk water-transport resistances in PFSA membranes are governed by distinct mechanisms: interfacial resistance falls as side-chain length increases, while bulk resistance is lowest for the medium side-chain (3M) chemistry.
