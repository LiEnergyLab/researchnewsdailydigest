### [Beyond Intrinsic Size Effects: Regulating Multiphase Transport and Interfacial Microenvironments within Catalyst Layers for CO2 Electrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02951)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolysis MEA · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-14 · **Authors:** Hu Zhang, Ruiyang Fan, Zhongyin Kang, Min Zhang, Qian Fu, Xun Zhu

Using cubic Cu2O particles of varying sizes in MEA CO2 electrolysis, the authors report a volcano-shaped relationship between particle size and C2+ selectivity, peaking at 70% for an intermediate size (Cu2O-3). ### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt/water balance · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance for CO2 electrolyzer cathodes shows that inlet CO2 from a water bubbler is often undersaturated, so evaporation inside the cathode chamber lowers the local liquid water-to-K+ ratio and drives bicarbonate salt precipitation—even though anolyte crossover water would otherwise suffice to dissolve the cotransported K+. Over-humidifying suppresses salt formation but causes electrode flooding, so water management must be balanced rather than maximized.

### [Three-Dimensionally Fibrillated Inorganic Composite Anion Exchange Membranes for Ampere-Scale Hydrogen Production](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02666)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** AEM alkaline water electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-22 · **Authors:** Mengjie Zhang, Qi Kong, Jing Hu, Shuhao Wang, Mengmeng Yang, Ibrahem O. Baibars

A free-standing anion exchange membrane made of 90 wt% layered double hydroxide in a fibrillated PTFE scaffold shows zero swelling and 0.089 Ω cm² area-specific resistance, and survived 1344 h in 1 M and 30 wt% KOH at 80 °C where commercial polymer membranes fail. It sustained 1 A cm⁻² for 550 h in an AEM electrolyzer and 425 h in an alkaline electrolyzer, with AIMD attributing OH⁻ transport to a confined hydrogen-bond network favoring Grotthuss hopping. The main caveat is that the 1 A cm⁻² durability tests are far shorter than the 1344 h ex-situ KOH stability, so long-term ampere-scale operation remains to be shown.
