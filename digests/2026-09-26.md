### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR acidic · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max Joshua Hülsey, Bryan Y. Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

CO₂ reduction on coinage metals in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted, meaning a local pH swing is a prerequisite for catalysis rather than a consequence of it. The authors show this by simultaneously tracking faradaic CO₂ consumption and non-faradaic (bi)carbonate formation with DEMS, and demonstrate that adding Pt to Ag to accelerate H₂ evolution lowers the CO₂RR onset overpotential by roughly 1 V. They propose that at low pH the *COOH intermediate fragments unproductively to CO₂ and H*, favoring H₂ evolution over CO₂ reduction.
