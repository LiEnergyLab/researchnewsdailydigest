**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electroreduction · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** Takashi Yamamoto, Taiga Ozawa, Mai Tomisaki, Yasuaki Einaga

Using an aqueous KClO₄ electrolyte instead of surface modification redirected boron-doped diamond's CO₂ reduction selectivity from formic acid to CO, reaching 72% Faradaic efficiency at −1.46 V vs RHE with 500 mL min⁻¹ flow at 5 °C. ### [Effects of pressure on the oxygen evolution reaction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.elecom.2026.108280)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** water electrolysis OER pressure · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-01 · **Authors:** Onno van der Heijden, Aleksandr Bashkatov, Alisson H.M. da Silva, Rafaël E. Vos, M. Albers, Detlef Lohse

Operating a three-electrode alkaline cell at 1–20 bar, the authors find OER current rises with pressure mainly because bubble-related non-kinetic losses are mitigated, with the enhancement scaling linearly against 1/p (volumetric gas flux) and leveling off at higher pressures. Current oscillations from large-bubble release are suppressed at elevated pressure while higher current persists, which the flux argument alone cannot explain—pointing to a pressure effect on the bubble departure mechanism itself. Practical takeaway: the OER gain from pressurization depends on how strongly a given system is limited by bubble removal.
