### [Green Hydrogen Production Using Low-Cost Alkaline Electrolysis Powered by Renewable Energy](https://doi.org/10.2118/234800-ms)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** alkaline electrolysis green hydrogen · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-10 · **Authors:** Ogechi Goodness Ibelegbu, Nkemakolam Chinedu Izuwa, Humphery Nwenenda Dike, Placid Ikechukwu Anyanwu, Odion Beston Akhabue, Ekwebelem Emmanuel Chinazor

In a lab-scale alkaline electrolyzer using KOH electrolyte and stainless-steel electrodes, hydrogen production only began at electrolyte concentrations of 400 g/L or higher, with the best yield (2.59 L) and Faradaic efficiency (61.9%) achieved at 600 g/L and 12 V.
