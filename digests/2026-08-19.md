### [Multi-fidelity computational screening of high-entropy MBenes for electrochemical CO2 reduction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-026-02287-4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR catalysts · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-17 · **Authors:** Sree Harsha Bharadwaj H, Raghavan Ranganathan

A multi-fidelity computational screen of 56 quinary high-entropy MBenes identified 18 viable CO2 reduction catalysts, with three compositions (CrNbZrMoTiB5, MoZrHfNbCrB5, MoZrHfTaCrB5) showing spontaneous CO2-to-CO conversion at zero applied potential, outperforming all previously reported MBene and high-entropy-alloy catalysts. The work provides a transferable screening framework for multicomponent 2D materials.

### [In-situ soft X-ray spectroscopy of OER metal oxide electrocatalysts for water splitting](https://doi.org/10.48683/1926.00130487>)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER water splitting · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** James J. C. Counter

Two new working electrode assemblies enable operando XPS and NEXAFS of OER catalysts, revealing that amorphous and semi-crystalline iridium oxides form mixed Ir4+/Ir5+ states and coordinatively unsaturated oxyl species under reaction conditions. ### [Experimental Study on Dynamic Performance of a 50 kW PEM Water Electrolysis System for Hydrogen Production](https://doi.org/10.3390/en19163844)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-17 · **Authors:** Guoqing Liu, Wei Xia, Guozheng Wang, Xiaojun Zhao, Song Hu, Haicheng Fu

In a 50 kW PEM electrolyzer, voltage responds to load changes in seconds, but thermal and gas-composition dynamics are much slower, taking ~34 minutes to reach rated temperature from cold start and exhibiting minute-scale delays in gas purity. Operationally, raising pressure from 1.2 to 2.9 MPa increased hydrogen-in-oxygen from 0.383% to 0.545%, while raising current from 300 to 1200 A cut oxygen-in-hydrogen from ~1002 ppm to ~6 ppm; full-load system power draw was 69.7 kW. These experimental data quantify the distinct response timescales for electrical, thermal, and purity variables, providing a practical basis for control strategies under variable renewable input.

### [A Crystallographic Descriptor for Alkaline Hydrogen Evolution on Nickel](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.6c04453)

On polycrystalline nickel, alkaline hydrogen evolution activity varies strongly by grain orientation, and the minimum angular deviation from the {101} pole is the best single crystallographic predictor of intrinsic activity. The paper argues that designing CO₂ electrolyzers to tolerate dilute or impure feeds simply shifts the separation burden downstream, where it can become more difficult or hazardous. ### [Multidimensional Design of Metal-Nitrogen-Carbon Electrocatalysts for Direct Propylene Epoxidation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14351v1)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Tag:** electrochemical epoxidation · **Journal:** arXiv · **Published:** 2026-08-14 · **Authors:** Songbo Ye, Qingyuan Han, Jingwen Chi, Yuan Huang, Heng Liu, Di Zhang, Hitoshi Shiku, Li Wei, Hao Li

Propylene epoxidation selectivity in metal-nitrogen-carbon catalysts is governed by a combination of oxygen binding energy, the potential of zero charge, and applied potential, not by oxygen binding alone. The work establishes interfacial electrostatics as an independent lever for tuning selective oxygen-atom transfer in electrocatalysis.
