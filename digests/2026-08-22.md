### [Getting real about transient electrocatalysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-026-01583-y)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Tag:** transient electrocatalysis · **Journal:** Nature Catalysis · **Published:** 2026-08-20 · **Authors:** Mijndert van der Spek

Dynamic operation of electrocatalytic systems—switching off when electricity prices spike—can improve process economics, but only in markets with large price fluctuations, and it typically causes severe catalyst degradation. A controlled power-down strategy tested over 750 hours, however, achieved negligible degradation, suggesting that smart shutdown protocols can make intermittent operation viable. The key takeaway is that the economic benefit is market-dependent, not universal.

Copper catalysts in flow-cell CO₂ electrolyzers can reduce CO₂ via the Eley–Rideal mechanism, where gaseous CO reacts directly with an adsorbed intermediate, rather than only through the traditionally assumed Langmuir–Hinshelwood pathway.
