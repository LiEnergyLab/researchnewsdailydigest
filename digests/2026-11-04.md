### [Green Hydrogen will Only Reach Cost Parity Once Electrolyzers are Engineered to Survive Cheap, Intermittent Renewable Electricity](https://doi.org/10.2118/235731-ms)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** electrolyzer intermittency · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Hen Dotan, Rotem Arad, Anan Garzuzi, Daniel Raam

Cheap renewable electricity alone won't deliver cost-parity green hydrogen because conventional alkaline and PEM electrolyzers degrade under the intermittent, on/off operation that low-cost solar and wind impose. A multiphysics model links electrolyte composition, pressure, and membrane properties to local cation concentration, pH, and CO2 availability in zero-gap CO2 electrolyzers, explaining non-intuitive effects such as thicker membranes increasing cation crossover via migration/diffusion flux balances.
