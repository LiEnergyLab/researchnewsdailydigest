### [Beyond Molecular Dispersion: A Molecular-Cone Architecture of Cobalt Phthalocyanine on CNTs for Selective CO2 Electroreduction to Methanol](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.6c04116)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR to methanol · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-15 · **Authors:** Yi-Xiang Wang, Rui Teng, Yunshuyu Sun, Meng Zhang, Yuxiang Mao, R Y

A CNT-supported nitro-substituted cobalt phthalocyanine catalyst arranged in a "molecular-cone" configuration avoids the aggregation that normally limits high-loading CO2-to-methanol electrocatalysts, achieving 64.4% methanol Faradaic efficiency at –0.98 V vs RHE and retaining 58.4% at 150 mA cm–2 in a membrane electrode assembly. The cone structure enhances local electric fields and K+ enrichment, which stabilizes the *CO intermediate and reorganizes interfacial water to speed up proton transport and hydrogenation kinetics. This is a microenvironment-engineering approach that decouples catalyst loading from selectivity loss.

PFSA ionomer adsorption onto catalyst particles is electrostatically self-limiting, with the extent of adsorption varying by nearly an order of magnitude across different particle types (e.g., carbon, Pt/C, IrOx).
