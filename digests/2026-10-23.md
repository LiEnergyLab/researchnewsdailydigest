### [Crossover breakdown analysis: Impact of mass and charge transport processes on H2 crossover in PEM water electrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2026.158258)

A 1D coupled mass/charge-transport model shows that H2 crossover in PEM water electrolysis rises with current density mainly because dissolved H2 transfers into cathode catalyst layer pores, supersaturating the ionomer; pore transport of H2 adds a second, smaller increase.
