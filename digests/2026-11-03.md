### [In-situ soft X-ray spectroscopy of OER metal oxide electrocatalysts for water splitting](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/130487/1/Counter_Thesis.pdf)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER electrocatalysts · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** James J. C. Counter

The study reports operando XPS/NEXAFS measurements on iridium and mixed Ru–Ir oxide OER catalysts using two new working-electrode assemblies that sustain ionic conductivity while allowing photoelectron detection. Amorphous and semi-crystalline IrOx showed mixed Ir4+/Ir5+ states and coordinatively unsaturated oxyl species under OER conditions, and potential-programmed NEXAFS resolved three oxygen species with distinct onset potentials, including an oxygen redox process forming electrophilic oxygen without further Ir5+ oxidation. ### [Green Hydrogen will Only Reach Cost Parity Once Electrolyzers are Engineered to Survive Cheap, Intermittent Renewable Electricity](https://doi.org/10.2118/235731-ms)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** green H2 electrolyzers · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** Hen Dotan, Rotem Arad, Anan Garzuzi, Daniel Raam

Cheap renewable electricity alone won't deliver cost-parity green hydrogen because conventional electrolyzers weren't designed for intermittent, curtailed, or behind-the-meter power. Under variable load, alkaline systems lose efficiency to shunt currents and gas crossover, while PEM systems suffer accelerated Ir/Pt catalyst dissolution and membrane thinning; repeated on/off cycling adds reverse-current corrosion in alkaline and idle-period gas accumulation in PEM. The authors argue electrolyzers must be engineered specifically for dynamic operation, and present decoupled water electrolysis data as one path.

### [Alkaline or PEM: Electrolyzer Selection for Continuous Hydrogen Supply Under Intermittent Renewable Power](https://doi.org/10.2118/235964-ms)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** electrolyzer selection · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Authors:** R. Srivastava

A pre-feasibility model for a 40 KTPA continuous refinery hydrogen plant in India finds alkaline electrolysis cheaper than PEM at current published costs—roughly INR 327/kg versus INR 415/kg LCOH, a ~27% advantage—using 54 vs 52 kWh/kg H2 and installed CAPEX of ~USD 465/kW vs USD 1,100–1,800/kW. PEM remains a candidate only for its faster dynamic response and wider operating range under high renewable variability, or if domestic manufacturing lowers future CAPEX. The comparison rests on literature-derived screening values and a single incentive scenario, so treat the cost gap as illustrative rather than definitive.

### [Inducing Electronic Redistribution in High-Entropy Spinel Oxides to Boost Water Oxidation](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.6c08228)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** AEMWE catalyst · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-01 · **Authors:** Yifan Zhao, Qiuyun Liu, Yangyang Yu, Shaodong Sun, Lingfeng Yang, Min Xi

Adding Ni, Mn, Cu, and Zn into a Co₃O₄ spinel lattice expands the lattice and shifts electron density toward higher oxidation states at Ni/Co sites, which promotes early formation of the oxyhydroxide OER-active phase. The resulting (NiMnCuZn)Co₂O₄ needs 225 mV overpotential at 10 mA cm⁻² and 299 mV at 100 mA cm⁻², beats commercial RuO₂, and holds stable operation for 700 h (500 h at 1 A cm⁻² in an AEM electrolyzer at 1.82 V). The work is a fairly standard high-entropy/multi-cation doping study with solid but incremental performance gains.

### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt formation · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance for CO2 electrolyzer cathodes shows that inlet CO2 from a water bubbler is often not fully humidified, so evaporation inside the cathode lowers the local liquid water-to-K+ ratio and triggers bicarbonate salt precipitation—even when anolyte crossover water would otherwise suffice to dissolve the K+. Excessive humidification prevents salt but causes electrode flooding, a direct trade-off the authors frame as an engineering guideline for water management.

### [Three-Dimensionally Fibrillated Inorganic Composite Anion Exchange Membranes for Ampere-Scale Hydrogen Production](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02666)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** AEM hydrogen production · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-22 · **Authors:** Mengjie Zhang, Qi Kong, Jing Hu, Shuhao Wang, Mengmeng Yang, Ibrahem O. Baibars

A free-standing anion exchange membrane made of 90 wt% layered double hydroxide in a fibrillated PTFE scaffold shows zero swelling and 0.089 Ω cm² area-specific resistance, and it survived 1,344 h in 1 M and 30 wt% KOH at 80 °C where commercial polymer membranes fail. The membrane sustained 1 A cm⁻² for 550 h in an AEM electrolyzer and 425 h in an alkaline electrolyzer, with AIMD attributing OH⁻ transport to a confined hydrogen-bond network favoring Grotthuss hopping.

### [Beyond Faradaic Efficiency: Towards efficient electroreduction of CO2 in Membrane-Electrode assemblies](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mattod.2026.103537)
