### [Symmetry-breaking induced highly active Ni sites simultaneously trigger interfacial hydrogen-bond network reconstruction for efficient CO2 electroreduction in strong acidic media](https://doi.org/10.1093/nsr/nwag701)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** acidic CO2RR · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-17 · **Authors:** Kai Song, Jiaqiang Lu, Yue Jing, Fengli Wei, Huizhu Cai, Xue Zhang

A Ni-based covalent organic polymer with symmetry-broken Ni sites (Ni vacancies plus uncoordinated pyrrolic N, confirmed by STM) reaches 97.5% CO Faradaic efficiency and 402.9 mA cm⁻² partial current density for CO2 reduction at pH 1, stable for 85 h without salt precipitation. The authors attribute the performance to the asymmetric sites suppressing HER and to uncoordinated pyrrolic N restructuring the interfacial hydrogen-bond network to limit proton transfer while enriching local CO2. The claim rests on one catalyst system in a single electrolyte; the mechanistic attribution is plausible but not independently validated here.

### [Dynamic Electrocatalysis Enhances the Oxygen Evolution Reaction Activity of Iridium Oxides](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.70341)

Square-wave potential modulation of iridium oxide anodes in acid boosts OER activity by up to 480% versus static operation. ### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

In CO₂ electrolyzers, salt precipitation is driven mainly by evaporation of liquid water inside the cathode chamber, since inlet CO₂ through a bubbler is often not fully water-saturated; this lowers the local water-to-K⁺ ratio enough to crystallize bicarbonate even when anolyte crossover would otherwise supply sufficient water. The authors' mass-balance framework shows a trade-off: extra humidification prevents salt but causes electrode flooding, so water management—not just salt solubility—governs stability.
