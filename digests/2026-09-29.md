Alloying Cu with Sn—and using an in-situ-formed oxidized Sn layer to block Cu oxidation/dissolution—suppressed surface reconstruction during CO2RR, yielding 93% CO Faradaic efficiency at 0.58 V overpotential and a 0.4% h⁻¹ current decay rate (about 14× more durable than pristine Cu). ### [Resolving Active-Site Heterogeneity in Fe–N–C Catalysts by Integrated Multimodal Spectroscopy and Simulation](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02124)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** Fe-N-C ORR catalysts · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-22 · **Authors:** Jiayi Xu, Prajay Patel, Matthew Edward Sweers, Esen E. Alp, Hoon T Chung, Piotr Zelenay

Combining Fe K-edge XAS, 57Fe Mössbauer spectroscopy, and DFT-assisted spectral simulations, the authors show that as-pyrolyzed Fe–N–C catalysts contain a heterogeneous mix of FeN4 sites—mainly a defect-associated pyrrolic motif with third-shell nitrogen coordination plus a smaller pyridinic fraction—rather than a single uniform site. ### [Revisiting Electrolyte Effects in Oxygen Evolution Reaction on NiOOH: pH‐Controlled Activity Depends on Catalyst Mesostructure](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.70300)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** NiOOH OER electrolyte · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-09 · **Authors:** Jordy J. J. Eggebeen, Thomas Touzalin, Yaxin Liu, Onno van der Heijden, Alfred Larsson, Marc T. M. Koper

The intrinsic OER activity of NiOOH depends on pH and alkali cation identity but not on bulk cation concentration; cation concentration only improves Ni(OH)₂/NiOOH redox reversibility by aiding ion transport in mesopores. The widely reported pH dependence is therefore largely a transport artifact—strongest in thick, heterogeneous films and minimal in thin, homogeneous ones.
