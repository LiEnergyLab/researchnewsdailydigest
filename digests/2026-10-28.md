### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2-electrolyzer-salt · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance for CO2 electrolyzer cathodes shows that inlet CO2 from a water bubbler is often undersaturated, so evaporation inside the cathode chamber lowers the local liquid-water-to-K+ ratio and triggers bicarbonate salt precipitation—even when anolyte crossover supplies enough water to dissolve the cotransported K+. Over-humidifying to prevent salt instead causes electrode flooding, a direct trade-off. The finding frames cathode water activity, not just crossover flux, as the controlling variable for salt management.
