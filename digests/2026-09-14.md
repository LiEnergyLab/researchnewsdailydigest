### [Consequences of overtones in Raman spectra for assigning nickel anode surface structures as NiO 2 during alkaline electrolysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-026-01613-9)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** alkaline-electrolysis · **Journal:** Nature Catalysis · **Published:** 2026-09-11 · **Authors:** Albert K. Engstfeld

In situ SERS and DFT show that the active surface of Ni(OH)₂ anodes under water oxidation is NiO₂-terminated, not NiOOH as commonly assumed.
