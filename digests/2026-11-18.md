### [Nanostructure-controlled OER performance and durability of sputtered ionomer-free RuO2 in PEM water electrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.241827)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM electrolysis RuO2 OER · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-16 · **Authors:** Martin Krammer, Stefan Edinger, Laura L. E. Mears, Matthias Kogler, Maximilian Weiß, Markus Valtiner

Sputtered, ionomer-free RuO₂ OER electrodes with smaller grains showed higher electrochemically active surface area and better performance in both three-electrode and PEM electrolyzer tests, which the authors attribute to proton-conductive hydrated grain boundaries. An optimized electrode at 0.64 mg cm⁻² loading ran >700 h at 1 A cm⁻², including >300 h at a degradation rate of 4 μV h⁻¹. The work supports nanostructure tuning of sputtered RuO₂ as a route to durable, scalable ionomer-free OER layers.

### [Atomic Layer Deposited Zirconium Dioxide/PTFE Separators for Intermediate‐Temperature Alkaline Water Electrolysis: In Situ Characterization](https://doi.org/10.1002/cite.70193)

ALD-ZrO₂-coated PTFE separators maintained stable area-specific resistance through heating/cooling cycles to 175°C in 45 wt% KOH at 30 bar, with fully reversible resistance recovery and H₂-in-O₂ below the 2 vol% safety limit down to 50 mA cm⁻². ### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt formation · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

A quantitative water balance for CO2 electrolyzer cathodes shows that inlet CO2 from a water bubbler is often undersaturated, so evaporation inside the cathode chamber lowers the local liquid water-to-K+ ratio and triggers bicarbonate salt precipitation—even though anolyte crossover would otherwise supply enough water to dissolve the K+. Excessive humidification prevents salt formation but causes electrode flooding, so the authors frame cathode water activity as the governing trade-off for stability.

A free-standing AEM with 90 wt% layered double hydroxide in a fibrillated PTFE scaffold shows zero swelling, low area resistance (0.089 Ω cm²), and 1344 h stability in 1 M and 30 wt% KOH at 80 °C.
