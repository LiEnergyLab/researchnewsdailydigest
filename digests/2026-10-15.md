### [In-situ soft X-ray spectroscopy of OER metal oxide electrocatalysts for water splitting](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/130487/1/Counter_Thesis.pdf)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER-electrocatalysts · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2027-01-01 · **Authors:** James J. C. Counter

Two new working-electrode designs enable operando XPS/NEXAFS of OER catalysts under realistic conditions, revealing that amorphous and semi-crystalline Ir oxides form mixed Ir⁴⁺/Ir⁵⁺ states and coordinatively unsaturated oxyl species during OER, with Ir 5d valence-band changes not previously reported.
