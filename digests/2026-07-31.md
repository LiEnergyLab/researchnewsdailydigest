### [The CO2 Concentration Inversion Effect: Definition, Quantitative Mechanistic Basis, and Design Rules for Dilute CO2 Electroreduction](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15006687/v1)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electroreduction · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-07-29 · **Authors:** Qing Yu, Ying Zhang, Peng Zhu, Qiankang Zhang

Under dilute CO₂ feeds (25–50% CO₂), copper-based catalysts can achieve higher C₂+ selectivity than with pure CO₂, a phenomenon termed the CO₂ Concentration Inversion Effect. This provides a practical design rule for directly converting industrial flue gas into multi-carbon products.

In alkaline water electrolysis flow cells, oxygen bubbles are "sticky" and coalesce into larger diameters at high current densities, while hydrogen bubbles remain small and glide along the electrode surface.
