### [Effects of pressure on the oxygen evolution reaction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.elecom.2026.108280)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** OER pressure electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-01 · **Authors:** Onno van der Heijden, Aleksandr Bashkatov, Alisson H.M. da Silva, Rafaël E. Vos, M. Albers, Detlef Lohse

In a 1–20 bar three-electrode alkaline cell, OER current under transient-bubble cyclic voltammetry rises with pressure mainly through mitigation of non-kinetic (bubble) effects, scaling linearly with 1/p and leveling off at higher pressures. The practical OER gain therefore depends on how strongly a given system is limited by bubble removal.
