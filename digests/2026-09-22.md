### [Consequences of overtones in Raman spectra for assigning nickel anode surface structures as NiO 2 during alkaline electrolysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-026-01613-9)

### [Managing Alkaline CO2 Electrolyzer Flooding using a Grooved Cathode Gas Diffusion Electrode](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c01086)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2-electrolyzer · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-08-25 · **Authors:** Aida Farsi, Vasant Batta, Jason K.D. Chan, Rui Kai Miao, Prasad V. Sarma, David Sinton

A grooved cathode GDE with through-plane holes at the electrode edges reduced CO Faradaic efficiency loss from 38.4% to 18.1% over 6 h at 250 mA/cm² under wet CO2, compared with a standard GDE. Under dry CO2, the grooves lowered cathode mass-transport resistance from 1.3 to 0.62 Ω·cm² and limited FE decline to 29.8% versus 35.5%. Pore-network modeling attributes the gains to lower anolyte saturation in the grooved MPL (61% vs. 82%) via preferential drainage through the grooves and holes.

### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR acidic electrolytes · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max J. Hülsey, Bryan Y. Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

CO2 reduction on coinage metals in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted, meaning a local pH swing is a prerequisite for the reaction rather than a consequence of it. The authors propose that at low local pH the *COOH intermediate instead fragments back to CO2 and H*, feeding H2 evolution rather than CO2RR, and show that promoting hydronium consumption (Pt-doped Ag) lowers the CO2RR onset overpotential by ~1 V.

### [Unifying Water Oxidation Chemistry of Iridium Oxide from Aqueous Model Systems to Proton Exchange Membrane Electrolysers](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15008370/v1)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** PEM-electrolysis · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-06 · **Authors:** Caiwu Liang, James Murawski, Cindy Tseng, Yue Wen, Yemin Tao, Chris Zalitis

Using identical IrOx catalysts across three-electrode, catalyst–ionomer, and MEA configurations, the authors show that Nafion ionomer does not change the intrinsic Ir redox transitions or the electrophilic oxygen species responsible for O–O bond formation—it mainly affects mass transport via oxygen trapping and microbubble accumulation. Operando Ir L3-edge XAS indicates higher average Ir oxidation states in MEAs than in aqueous electrodes, attributed to better catalyst utilization rather than different chemistry, and open-circuit decay shows IrOx persists in high oxidation states for hours without hydrogen crossover. The work frames catalyst utilization and transport, not reaction mechanism, as the main source of MEA-versus-aqueous discrepancies.
