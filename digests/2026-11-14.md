### [Competing Surface Dynamics of Copper Catalyst Particles during Electrochemical Carbon Dioxide Reduction](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnano.6c11607)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR Cu catalysts · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-12 · **Authors:** Yifan Chen, Tom Burwell, Madasamy Thangamuthu, Sadegh Ghaderzadeh, Gazi N. Aliev, Haytham E. M. Hussein

Using identical-location TEM on individual Cu nanoparticles on graphitized carbon nanofibers, the authors observed Ostwald ripening, coalescence, selective etching, and charge-driven fragmentation coexisting in the same region under CO2RR, with fragmentation seen in particles >10 nm. The work maps surface-confined restructuring pathways relevant to Cu catalyst stability.

### [Relating Cu Phase Transitions to Selectivity Recovery During Pulsed CO2 Reduction on Gas Diffusion Electrodes](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.6c06124)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** pulsed CO2RR GDE · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-11-11 · **Authors:** Finn Beiglboeck, Fabio Furcas, Natalie Frassl, Davide Salusso, Adrian Wichser, Sophia Haussener

Pulsed CO₂ reduction on Cu gas-diffusion electrodes can recover C2+ selectivity via distinct mechanisms depending on pulse speed and electrolyte: fast pulses at locally alkaline pH recover C2+ and CO together, while quasi-adiabatic pulses recover CO independently of C2+. In acidic KCl, C2+ recovery occurs through Cu dissolution/redeposition without any Cu oxide phase forming, showing oxide formation is not required for C–C coupling. The authors map potential–pH trajectories onto Cu phase diagrams to rationalize pulse design.

Each HER transition in an Ag CO2-to-CO electrolyser shifts resistance out of the 20–250 Hz charge-transfer band into slower 0.1–20 Hz CO2 transport responses, mostly at the cathode, with incomplete recovery accumulating over successive cycles.
