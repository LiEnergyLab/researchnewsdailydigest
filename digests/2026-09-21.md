### [Managing Alkaline CO2 Electrolyzer Flooding using a Grooved Cathode Gas Diffusion Electrode](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.6c01086)

A grooved cathode GDE with edge through-holes reduced CO Faradaic efficiency loss from 38.4% to 18.1% over 6 h at 250 mA/cm² under wet CO₂, and from 35.5% to 29.8% under dry CO₂, by draining anolyte and lowering cathode mass-transport resistance (0.62 vs. 1.3 Ω·cm²). ### [Acidic local environments inhibit CO2 reduction catalysis on coinage metals](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-kpc2c/v4)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2RR acidic electrolytes · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-09-10 · **Authors:** Max J. Hülsey, Bryan Y. Tang, Sophia Weng, Yogesh Surendranath

Using real-time DEMS and scanning voltammetry on coinage metals, the authors show that CO₂ reduction in acidic electrolyte only begins once interfacial hydronium is substantially depleted—i.e., a local pH swing is a prerequisite, not a consequence, of CO₂RR. They propose that at low local pH, the *COOH intermediate fragments unproductively to CO₂ and H*, favoring H₂ evolution, which explains why spectator cations are required to establish a hydronium-depleted interface. Doping Ag with Pt to accelerate H₂ evolution lowered the CO₂RR onset overpotential by ~1 V, consistent with that picture.

### [Unifying Water Oxidation Chemistry of Iridium Oxide from Aqueous Model Systems to Proton Exchange Membrane Electrolysers](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15008370/v1)

Using identical IrOx catalysts across three-electrode, catalyst–ionomer, and MEA configurations, the authors show that Nafion ionomer does not change the intrinsic Ir redox transitions or the electrophilic oxygen species responsible for O–O bond formation; it mainly affects mass transport via oxygen trapping and microbubble accumulation.
