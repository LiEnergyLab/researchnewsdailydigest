Two atomically precise S@Cu14 nanoclusters differing only in the position of a methyl group on their thiolate ligands show markedly different CO2RR behavior: the 3-MBT variant reaches 52% formate and 18% CO Faradaic efficiency at −1.4 V vs RHE with suppressed HER and remains structurally intact post-catalysis, while the 4-MBT analog is less selective and degrades with ligand loss. ### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)
**Score:** 10/10 · **Tag:** CO2 electrolyzer salt/water · **Journal:** OpenAlex · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Authors:** Yuge Feng, Shaoyun Hao, Zhou Yu, Ziteng Zuo, Tae‐Ung Wi, Ahmad Elgazzar

Inlet CO₂ streams through a water bubbler are often not fully humidified, so significant evaporation occurs inside the cathode chamber, lowering the local liquid water-to-K⁺ ratio and driving bicarbonate salt precipitation even when anolyte crossover water would otherwise suffice to dissolve the cotransported K⁺. The practical lever is tighter control of cathode-chamber water activity rather than relying on bubbler humidification alone.
