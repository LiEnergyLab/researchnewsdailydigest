### [Unraveling Water Balances behind Salt Formation in CO2 Reduction Electrolyzers](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c12691)

A quantitative water balance for CO2 electrolyzer cathodes shows that inlet CO2 from a water bubbler is often not fully humidified, so evaporation inside the cathode chamber lowers the local liquid water-to-K+ ratio and drives bicarbonate salt precipitation—even though anolyte crossover water would otherwise suffice to dissolve the cotransported K+.
